## Build

1. **TouchKit fills `TouchFrame.buttonDown`.** The multitouch frame callback has no button field. Pick the most reliable source you can verify and justify it in the done report (e.g. a listen-only event tap for left mouse down/up/dragged that keeps a thread-safe flag, or sampling the pressed-buttons state per frame). Requirements: the flag must be correct by the time the frame that follows the press is handed out; works for the built-in trackpad and a Bluetooth trackpad; a failed tap install degrades to `false` with one logged reason; nothing installed at construction (lazy, on `start`), removed on `stop`. Put the live source behind an internal protocol with a fake. The trace recorder stores `buttonDown`.
2. **Gestures reject clicks.** A candidate that sees `buttonDown` at any frame before it claims is rejected with `.physicalClick` and never claims afterwards (the touch stays not-ours until lift). A live claim (slide or hold) that sees `buttonDown` is cancelled the way a palm cancels it (`.cancelled` phase, claim released, hold gets its up). A tap/double-tap/swipe whose touch clicked never fires. Enable `physicalClickAndDragStartingInAnEdgeStripIsNotOurs` (extend `TraceBuilder` to emit `buttonDown`); add: click without drag in a corner (no tap), click mid-slide (cancel), click after a long-press began (hold ends), force-click style long press (button down while still) → not a long-press.
3. **Tap waits for double-tap.** When a zone is in both the tap-bound set and `context.doubleTapZones` (tap-bound = enabled zones without a slide-only/swipe-only reading — derive it exactly; document), a single tap is held until the double-tap window (`settings.doubleTapWindowMS`) passes with no second tap, then emitted as `.tap` with its original position; a second tap inside the window emits only `.doubleTap`. Zones not in `doubleTapZones` keep firing taps immediately (no added latency). `doubleTapZones == nil` = nobody waits. The pending tap must be emitted on a later `process` call driven by time — find how the pipeline advances time today (frames keep coming while a finger is down, but not after lift) and add what's needed (e.g. emit on the next frame from that device, plus an explicit `func flush(now:)` or similar additive API on the recognizer; if that needs the frozen `GestureRecognizing` protocol to change, write a blocker and propose the smallest addition instead). Reset/pause drops a pending tap.
4. **Corner size independence.** Make every gesture test that depends on corner size use explicit `GestureSettings` (not `.defaults`), so the chief can change the default 12 → 10 mm without breaking tests. Add a test pair proving the boundary moves with `cornerSizeMM` (12 vs 10). Re-run the adversarial suite at 10 mm and report anything that changes.
5. `scripts/record-traces.md`: add scenarios for click-drag along each edge, click in a corner, tap-vs-double-tap on a zone with both bound.
