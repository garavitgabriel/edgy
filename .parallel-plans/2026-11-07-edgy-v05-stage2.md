## Hosting (each batch owns its controller, AppKit inside EdgyUI)
- **17:** `MenuBarController` (`@MainActor`, `init(model:)`, `install()`/`uninstall()`): the `NSStatusItem` with the glyph for the current `MenuGlyph.State`, and an `NSPopover` (transient, glass) hosting `MenuPopoverView`. The list's actions call closures the app passes in (`MenuBarActions`: openSettings, openConfigFile, reloadConfig, about, quit) — define that struct in your folder.
- **18:** `OverlayController` (`@MainActor`, `init(model:)`, `start()`/`stop()`): a non-activating, click-through `NSPanel` per screen (never takes focus, ignores mouse except the display-check plate), fed from `model.takeDecisions()` on the main run loop; plate docking at the touched edge/corner, fully on-screen.
- **19:** `OnboardingController` (`init(model:actions:)`, `show()`): the 640 × 520 glass window; finishes by calling a closure (the app marks onboarding done and opens the popover once).
The chief wires the controllers in EdgyApp after merge.
