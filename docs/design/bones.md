# Bones — Edgy (DRAFT, pre-round-1)

Written from the product constraints and the frozen data model on 2026-11-04, before any render exists. Round-1 critics will confirm, kill or add to this list. Every bone has to be something you can check by looking at a render.

## Hard structural rules (draft)

B1. **The mirror is the pad.** It's drawn to scale (real aspect ratio) and in the same orientation as the trackpad under the user's hands. Left on screen is left under the fingers.
B2. **Zone types read at a glance.** Edges, segments and corners can be told apart in a blurred thumbnail. A mapped corner (it wins) and a pass-through corner (it falls to its edge) look different.
B3. **Recognized vs rejected is the main signal.** A recognized touch and a rejected touch look clearly different. A rejection names its reason (one of the 8) in plain words, next to the touch.
B4. **One accent means "live".** The accent marks a recognized touch or the active zone and nothing else. It doesn't go on buttons, headers or decoration.
B5. **The binding chain is flat.** Zone → gesture → action → parameters is editable in place, with no modal chain. The action picker scales past 8 actions.
B6. **The overlay never takes focus.** It's click-through and sits only at the perimeter or as a small panel. Live work underneath stays readable.
B7. **The menu glyph is a template image.** Monochrome, it reads at 16 pt in a light and a dark menu bar, and it isn't a tiny copy of the app icon.

## Kill list (draft, pasted into LOCKED from round 1)

- Tracked micro-caps eyebrow labels above every block: the most common machine-made tell.
- A grey rounded rectangle with a soft drop shadow standing in for the trackpad: that's the genre default.
- N equal cards in a grid, one per zone, with no hierarchy.
- Gradient blobs, glassmorphism stacked on glassmorphism, neon glow on everything.
- Emoji or stock system symbols used as the only gesture vocabulary.
- One corner radius on every surface.
- Fake usage stats, fake testimonials, made-up percentages.
- Two-clause "oracular" copy ("Your edges. Your rules.").
- Any third-party product name, logo or look-alike.
