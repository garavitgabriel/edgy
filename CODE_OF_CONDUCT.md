# Code of conduct

Edgy's issues, pull requests and discussions are for making Edgy better. Everyone taking part is expected to keep them useful and decent.

## Expected

- Talk about the code, the design or the behaviour, not the person.
- Assume the other person meant well, and ask before you conclude they didn't.
- Give the detail that lets someone act: what you did, what happened, what you expected.
- Accept a "no" on a feature. Edgy stays small on purpose.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment or threats, in public or in private.
- Remarks about someone's identity, body, background or experience level.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressing a point after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## What happens

A maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close or lock a thread, or block an account, depending on how serious it is and whether it repeats.

To report a problem, write to hello@edgy.to. Reports are kept private.
